University Classroom Assignments

Cohorts of 80–300 computer science students simultaneously creating fresh accounts, fork-starring course templates, and committing code within the identical 2-hour lab deadline window.

Campus ASN Subnet Clustering: Ingestion of university IP CIDR blocks and IPEDS curriculum repository mapping to suppress organic classroom synchronized spikes.

Chinese Spring Festival & Diwali Sprints

Massive coordinated open source initiatives (e.g. "Year of Open Source" sprints) generating tightly bounded bursts of stargazers with newly minted accounts in East/South Asia timezones.
